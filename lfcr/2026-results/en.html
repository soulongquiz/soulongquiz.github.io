LFCR 2026 Results

April 6, 2026

After a very tight final, Cannes (Suu Cannes Tsu) won the 2026 French Riichi Clubs League! This victory qualifies the Cannes club for the Riichi Mahjong Champions League for the second year in a row.

Congratulations also to our Sou-Long Mahjong players, who finished second and kept us dreaming until the last minute of the competition!
