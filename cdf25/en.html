Results of the 2025 French Riichi Championship

January 16, 2026

The 2025 French Riichi Championship was held on December 13 and 14 in Toulouse. It was the first EMA tournament organized by our Sou-Long Mahjong club, but it fortunately took place under very good conditions thanks to the organizers, volunteers, referees, as well as the logistical support from Blue Frog Mahjong. Thank you!!

After two days of intense competition, it's our friend from Évry, Anthony, who wins the title of 2025 French Riichi Champion! Congratulations to him as well as to all the participants!
